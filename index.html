How it works, and what is real in this prototype
  • You do all the explaining. Nova never gives answers.
  • AI plays Nova and afterwards collects quotes from what you said, with a suggested level. Everything it writes is marked AI.
  • Your teacher sets the key ideas beforehand, reads the conversation and decides the score. You can add a note or ask for a live oral instead.
  • Works now: live conversation in five stages, evidence notes, teacher review and approval, topic setup, voice input and read-aloud where the browser allows. Kazakh, Russian or English.
  • Not built yet: accounts, Moodle link, central storage (records stay in this browser), group analytics.
